# Faculty Qualifications and Credentialing Portal

The Faculty Qualifications and Credentialing Portal is the page Bossier Parish Community College's academic deans use to document each faculty qualification determination under the College's Faculty Qualifications and Credentialing Policy, until the College's system of record for credentialing is in place. It runs entirely in the dean's browser and stores nothing. Each determination ends in one PDF packet saved to the dean's own computer.

After the download, the page offers a Submit for approval link that opens the College's approval form in Jotform with the record's details filled in. The dean attaches the packet there and submits it, and the Office of Academic Affairs routes it through approval and files the final record. A program director can instead prepare a packet for the academic dean or assistant dean to submit; the dean opens that PDF on this page and chooses Review and submit, which opens the same approval form with the dean as the submitter. When the AVCI denies an exception-based request, the dean can open that packet here and choose Request reconsideration, which builds a reconsideration packet and opens the College's reconsideration form in Jotform the same way. Once a record is approved, Academic Affairs opens the packet here and places the signed Approval Record from Jotform after the report before filing it. Nothing a dean enters or attaches is sent to this site, and the page sends nothing to Jotform on its own. The record's details travel only in those links, and only when the dean opens one.

`index.html` is built from the form's source, kept by the Office of Academic Affairs. Edit the source and rebuild rather than editing this file by hand.
