# Faculty Qualifications and Credentialing Portal

The Faculty Qualifications and Credentialing Portal is the page Bossier Parish Community College's academic deans use to document each faculty qualification determination under the College's Faculty Qualifications and Credentialing Policy, until the College's system of record for credentialing is in place. It runs entirely in the dean's browser. While a record is in progress, the page keeps it as a draft in that browser on that computer, attached files included, until its packet downloads or for 14 days after the last change, so the dean can close the page and pick up where they left off; it is kept nowhere else. Save draft downloads the record with its files, to finish on another computer. Each determination ends in one PDF packet saved to the dean's own computer.

After the download, the page offers a Submit for approval link that opens the College's approval form in Jotform with the record's details filled in. The dean attaches the packet there and submits it, and the Office of Academic Affairs routes it through approval and files the final record. A program director can instead prepare a packet for the academic dean or assistant dean to submit; the dean opens that PDF on this page and chooses Review and submit, which opens the same approval form with the dean as the submitter. When the AVCI denies an exception-based request, the dean can open that packet here and choose Request reconsideration, which builds a reconsideration packet and opens the College's reconsideration form in Jotform the same way. When a record's route in Jotform is complete, whatever its outcome, Academic Affairs opens the packet here, chooses Finish and file, and gives it the Decision Record from Jotform's filing email; the page draws its own Decision Record after the report, with each decision, its signature, and its date. Beside the final packet's download, File in SharePoint opens the College's filing form in Jotform with the record filled in, and Academic Affairs attaches the final packet there. Nothing a dean enters or attaches is sent to this site, and the page sends nothing to Jotform on its own. The record's details travel only in those links, and only when the dean opens one.

`index.html` is built from the form's source, kept by the Office of Academic Affairs. Edit the source and rebuild rather than editing this file by hand.
